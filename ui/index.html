Master Chat

All your inboxes and chats in one window. Each account runs in its own sealed browser profile, and the pages can't touch the app or your files.

Overview

Real-time accounts stay fully awake while hidden, so new messages notify at once. Untick one to let it rest and save memory; its messages may then arrive a few minutes late.

Recent messages

    Messages that arrive while Master Chat is open show up here, from every account, even muted ones or in Do Not Disturb. They're kept in memory only, never saved.
